Cultural insight

Kitto katsu — “surely win”

The brand name echoes a Japanese expression of success. Nestlé turned the association into exam-season encouragement.

ProductChocolate as an encouragement gift.
PackagingA mail-ready package for a personal message and address.
MarketingExam-season campaign through Japan Post.
Media Grand Prix
2009 Cannes Lions · confirmed by Japan Post.
Commercial opportunity ≠ proven sales increase. The award documents campaign recognition, not a measured demand effect. The lesson: adapt the meaning and the occasion, not just the product.
